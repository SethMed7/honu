Honu

Go ahead. Draw something.

Simulated desktop · Real little scribbles

Make your point.
Then disappear.

Built for the moment when “right here” needs a circle around it.

Instant overlay

Press ⌘ ⇧ D and your screen becomes a canvas. Sketch over a call, a deck, or your IDE. Toggle again to get back to it.

Zero persistence

No files generated, no cloud sync, no clutter. Clear on demand, or quit and leave every stroke behind.

Native & lightweight

Tauri v2 uses the webview already on your Mac. No bundled browser engine. Just Excalidraw’s familiar drawing feel.

Your pen, for the session

Color, stroke weight, and tool stay in memory across toggles. Pick your favorite once, then keep the conversation moving.

You’re all set.
